About

I like the moment code leaves the screen and something physical moves.

I'm an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at NYU Tandon, and I work across the whole stack of a robot: wiring and motor drivers, embedded control, navigation, perception, and the learning systems on top.

Recently that's meant leading hardware for an off-grid irrigation system now deployed as a paying customer pilot, building navigation for NYU's combat robotics team, and training manipulation tasks in NVIDIA Isaac Lab and MuJoCo. At CUNY I built a robot tutor that adapts to autistic children's emotions and presented it at the BARS Symposium.

I started in computer science at BMCC, graduating with honors, and spent a year as a CUNY Tech Prep fellow mentored by Amazon engineers. The thread through all of it: I want the things I build to work outside the lab.
